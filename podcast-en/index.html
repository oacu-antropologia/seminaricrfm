Edge

Critical ideas for an unequal world

A podcast hosted by Martin Lundsteen and Linn Biorklund, opening critical ideas to a wider audience without flattening their complexity. Each episode begins with a concrete question and a concept that helps us look again at how inequalities are produced, governed and contested.

The sound of Edge

Conversations with researchers are interwoven with exchanges between both hosts throughout each episode. Stories, objects and everyday situations connect research to work, mobility, housing, displacement, violence, care and solidarity. Questions, objections and connections between the hosts’ research keep the discussion moving.

The questions that drive us

Racial capitalism
How are race, class, labour and accumulation connected?
Borders and mobility
Who can move, who must stay, and how are rights distributed?
Coloniality and empire
How do colonial histories persist in contemporary institutions and inequalities?
Racial regimes and antiracism
What sustains racism when institutions proclaim equality?
Abolition
What does it mean to challenge control, confinement and violence?

Episode 1 · Racial Capitalism

With Arun Kundnani

What is racial capitalism — and what becomes visible when we understand race and capitalism as part of the same social order?

The episode opens with a deceptively simple question: how can a dress cost five pounds? Starting with this everyday object, the conversation explores how particular lives and forms of labour are made more precarious and cheaper.

  • The material power of racism and the limits of simply calling it “structural”.
  • South African apartheid, Cedric Robinson and Stuart Hall.
  • Neoliberalism, the postcolonial order and the limits of liberal antiracism.
  • Understanding race and class together without reducing one to the other.
  • Theory and activism: what can a concept help us see — and change?

The first episode is in preparation. Audio and listening links will be added when available.

The voices behind Edge

Martin Lundsteen and Linn Biorklund are researchers in the Department of Social Anthropology at the University of Barcelona and members of GER. Their research connects urban borders and inequalities with displacement, violence, care and solidarity in contexts of migration.
